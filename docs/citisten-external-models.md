## Runtime contract

`createWorld(canvas,{modelLOD:0})` is synchronous; await `world.ready` before
play, rendering or snapshots. Readiness rejects missing/failed resources and
there is no procedural fallback. LOD0 is default; explicitly choose 1 or 2 for
the entire session. Only one alternative is loaded. No hidden far blockout.

The [mesh API](citisten-buildings-api.md) lists all 17 keys, exact triangles,
instance-handle semantics and disposal. Static parts batch by material/layout;
rigid agents retain named hierarchy transforms. Actual pose movement advances
citizen/zombie walk loops and signed car travel rotates the four wheels.
Stationary agents do not advance animation. No doors, steering, services or
combat behavior is invented beyond existing simulation actions.

All five terrain materials are used: grass, soil and shoreline ground groups,
asphalt roads, and water. Imported base/normal/packed roughness-metalness
channels retain their factors and color spaces; UV sampler state is cloned,
not written into the canonical package. Existing analytic block AO is retained
because the delivery has no terrain occlusion textures. Water normals scroll
without moving geometry or changing occupancy.
