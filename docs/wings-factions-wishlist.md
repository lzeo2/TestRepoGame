# Wings: Separate Factions Mode Wishlist

## Scope and balance conventions

**Separate mode only, not the standard aircraft wishlist.** The [standard wishlist](wings-foreign-planes-wishlist.md) contains exactly 20 aircraft and no vessels. This document proposes a distinct Factions mode with carriers, large surface combatants and small boats alongside aircraft. It does not authorize implementation, registration, publication or push.

The complete proposed starting roster is **30 entries: 15 per faction, each with 10 aircraft and 5 vessels**. Every roster slot is listed below; there are no implied extra aircraft, escorts or carrier air wings. This is a curated game roster, not an exhaustive inventory of any country's armed forces or a promise to import every existing Wings vehicle.

- **Faction A: NATO-inspired coalition.** Primarily NATO-origin equipment, plus the South Korean FA-50 as an explicitly fictional partner contribution. South Korea is not a NATO member. Multinational aircraft programs do not imply that every partner operates every variant.
- **Faction B: Eastern-inspired coalition.** Russian/Soviet-origin, Chinese and jointly Pakistani-Chinese equipment. "Eastern Bloc" is only a historical inspiration, not an accurate name for a present-day alliance: the Soviet Union and Warsaw Pact no longer exist, and China and Pakistan were not Warsaw Pact members. These teams are fictional balance groupings, not claims about current alliances, interoperability or shared ownership.
- **Era:** Late Cold War designs still relevant to modern forces and newer platforms. Dates describe service entry of the variant or class, not first flight. Inclusion does not certify current readiness; Admiral Kuznetsov in particular is a historical/nominal roster choice, not a claim of operational availability.
- **Stats are proposals:** All speeds, weapon counts and handling scores are original arcade balance suggestions, not real performance figures, operational loadouts or capability rankings. Fixed-wing speed is cruise/boost in game m/s; helicopter and vessel speed is maximum game m/s without boost. Handling is 1-5 within each movement class, with 5 most responsive; a helicopter's 4/5 does not imply jet-like turns.
- **Weapons and implementation:** Counts are ready rounds or abstract channels, not total real magazines. Aircraft guided strike can reuse existing TV-missile/Maverick abstractions; aircraft air-to-air weapons retain current targeting limits. Bombers, revised helicopter fits, naval missiles, defensive mounts and water handling need separate design and testing. No radar, sonar, torpedo, nuclear weapon or classified capability simulation is promised.
- **Carriers:** Begin as AI fleet objectives, not promised playable ships or mobile spawn points. Escort control and small-boat play also require separate acceptance. No catapult, arresting gear, vertical landing, deck operations or automatic aircraft spawning is assumed. Land-based aircraft on a faction's roster are not its carrier air wing.
- **Existing content:** F-35 and Apache entries refine existing families rather than claiming they are absent. Existing generic fleet scenery and the experimental speedboat do not establish these named classes or a complete playable naval roster. See the [Wings manual](maintenance/games/232-wings.md) for source, rights, tooling and native-play holds.
- **NATO names:** Western service/product names are not NATO reporting names. Family reporting names are identified as such; uncertain public designations remain qualified.

## Faction A: NATO-inspired coalition (10 aircraft, 5 vessels)

### 1. General Dynamics F-16C Fighting Falcon

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Multirole fighter. **Era:** Mid-1980s service entry; modernized examples remain in service. **Origin country:** United States.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 150/210 m/s; armament: cannon, 2 short-range air-to-air missiles and 2 guided-strike rounds; handling 5/5, quick roll and strong medium-speed turns with low-speed energy loss.
- **Gameplay fit:** Supplies the coalition's accessible lightweight multirole fighter.

### 2. Lockheed Martin F-35A Lightning II

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Low-observable multirole fighter. **Era:** 2010s service entry. **Origin country:** United States, with international program partners.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 130/180 m/s; armament: existing arcade cannon and 2 guided-strike rounds per missile seat; handling 4/5, stable medium-speed turns with limited pursuit speed.
- **Gameplay fit:** Preserves the existing strike/stealth niche as a land-based variant, not a carrier aircraft.

The cannon20/TV-missile fit is an arcade abstraction, not a real F-35A gun caliber or operational inventory; retain existing stealth rules unless separately approved.

### 3. Eurofighter Typhoon

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Air-superiority and multirole fighter. **Era:** 2000s service entry. **Origin countries:** United Kingdom, Germany, Italy and Spain.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 155/215 m/s; armament: cannon and 4 air-to-air missiles; handling 5/5, rapid roll and strong high-speed turns with wider low-speed recovery.
- **Gameplay fit:** Provides fast fleet defense while giving up dedicated strike ammunition.

### 4. Dassault Rafale C

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Land-based multirole fighter. **Era:** 2000s service entry. **Origin country:** France.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 145/200 m/s; armament: cannon, 2 air-to-air missiles and 2 guided-strike rounds; handling 4/5, predictable low-speed control with moderate boost acceleration.
- **Gameplay fit:** Balances air cover and precision attack without implying that the C variant operates from carriers.

### 5. Saab JAS 39C Gripen

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Lightweight multirole fighter. **Era:** 2000s C-variant service entry; family entered service in the 1990s. **Origin country:** Sweden.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 140/195 m/s; armament: cannon, 2 air-to-air missiles and 2 guided-strike rounds; handling 5/5, fast roll and tight medium-speed turns with a lower boost ceiling.
- **Gameplay fit:** Gives the coalition a compact strike fighter that cannot outrun heavy interceptors.

### 6. Dassault Mirage 2000C

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Air-defense fighter. **Era:** 1980s service entry; wider family remains in modern use. **Origin country:** France.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 145/205 m/s; armament: cannon and 4 air-to-air missiles; handling 4/5, sharp initial turns with substantial sustained-turn speed loss.
- **Gameplay fit:** Offers a timing-focused interceptor distinct from the Typhoon's sustained high-speed response.

### 7. Korea Aerospace Industries FA-50 Fighting Eagle

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Light combat aircraft derived from an advanced trainer. **Era:** 2010s service entry. **Origin country:** South Korea, with United States industrial participation.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 125/175 m/s; armament: cannon, 2 short-range air-to-air missiles and 2 guided-strike rounds; handling 4/5, smooth roll and predictable turns with limited pursuit speed.
- **Gameplay fit:** Adds an explicitly fictional partner slot for approachable light combat.

### 8. Fairchild Republic A-10C Thunderbolt II

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Close-air-support attack aircraft. **Era:** 2000s C upgrade of a 1970s-service aircraft. **Origin country:** United States.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 90/120 m/s; armament: heavy cannon and 6 guided-strike rounds; handling 3/5, controlled low-speed turns with limited climb and escape speed.
- **Gameplay fit:** Gives ground-objective attacks sustained firepower at the cost of vulnerability to fighters.

### 9. BAE Systems Hawk T2 (Hawk 128)

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Advanced jet trainer. **Era:** Late-2000s service entry. **Origin country:** United Kingdom.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 110/150 m/s; armament: none in the proposed trainer fit; handling 5/5, forgiving roll and predictable medium-speed turns.
- **Gameplay fit:** Supports faction training and checkpoint races, not an invented frontline combat role.

### 10. Boeing AH-64D Apache Longbow

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable; Apache is a United States service name. **Role:** Attack helicopter. **Era:** Late-1990s D-variant service entry. **Origin country:** United States.
- **Suggested stats:** Maximum forward speed 75 m/s, no boost; armament: chain gun, 4 guided-strike rounds and 2 rocket salvos; handling 4/5, stable hover and responsive yaw with slow acceleration.
- **Gameplay fit:** Refines the existing Apache family for low-altitude cover and precision attack without promising radar simulation.

### 11. Charles de Gaulle (R91), Charles de Gaulle-class aircraft carrier

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Nuclear-powered aircraft carrier. **Era:** 2000s service entry. **Origin country:** France.
- **Suggested stats:** Maximum speed 14 m/s, no boost; armament: 2 abstract defensive missile channels and short-range defensive guns; handling 1/5, slow rudder response and very wide turns.
- **Gameplay fit:** Supplies a major fleet objective whose survival depends on escorts rather than a simulated air wing.

### 12. Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carrier

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Aircraft carrier designed for short-takeoff/vertical-landing aircraft. **Era:** 2010s service entry. **Origin country:** United Kingdom.
- **Suggested stats:** Maximum speed 13 m/s, no boost; armament: 3 abstract close-in defensive gun mounts, no offensive missiles; handling 1/5, slow acceleration and broad turns.

### 13. Type 45 Daring-class destroyer

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable; Daring is a class name. **Role:** Area-air-defense guided-missile destroyer. **Era:** 2000s service entry. **Origin country:** United Kingdom.
- **Suggested stats:** Maximum speed 16 m/s, no boost; armament: main gun, 4 ready anti-air missiles and 2 close-in defensive mounts; handling 2/5, deliberate turns with better acceleration than carriers.
- **Gameplay fit:** Makes escort suppression necessary before straightforward attacks on capital objectives.

### 14. Aquitaine-class FREMM frigate

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable. **Role:** Multimission frigate, using the Aquitaine antisubmarine variant as reference. **Era:** 2010s service entry. **Origin country:** France, within the joint French-Italian FREMM program.

- **Gameplay fit:** Fills a lighter escort tier without assuming sonar, torpedoes or antisubmarine gameplay exists.

### 15. Dockstavarvet Combat Boat 90 H (CB90 H)

- **NATO reporting name:** Not applicable; CB90 is a product designation. **Role:** Fast assault and troop-transport boat. **Era:** 1990s service entry. **Origin country:** Sweden.
- **Suggested stats:** Maximum speed 22 m/s, no boost; armament: 3 machine-gun positions, no missiles; handling 5/5, responsive waterjet-style steering with speed loss in tight turns.
- **Gameplay fit:** Supplies a lightly armed coastal objective runner without requiring troop simulation.

## Faction B: Eastern-inspired coalition (10 aircraft, 5 vessels)

### 16. Mikoyan MiG-29

- **NATO reporting name:** Fulcrum. **Role:** Frontline air-superiority fighter. **Era:** 1980s service entry; upgraded variants remain in modern service. **Origin country:** Soviet Union.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 145/205 m/s; armament: cannon and 4 short-range air-to-air missiles; handling 5/5, sharp initial turns with poor speed retention under sustained maneuvering.
- **Gameplay fit:** Supplies close-range defense that rewards energy management rather than a large strike load.

### 17. Sukhoi Su-35S

- **NATO reporting name:** Flanker-E. **Role:** Multirole air-superiority fighter. **Era:** 2010s service entry. **Origin country:** Russia.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 155/215 m/s; armament: cannon, 4 air-to-air missiles and 2 guided-strike rounds; handling 4/5, strong pitch authority with slower roll than light fighters.
- **Gameplay fit:** Combines air cover and strike flexibility with a larger, easier-to-hit silhouette.

### 18. Chengdu J-20

- **NATO reporting name:** Fagin is reported in public Western usage; official NATO confirmation is not established here. Mighty Dragon is a translated Chinese nickname, not a NATO reporting name. **Role:** Low-observable air-superiority fighter. **Era:** 2010s service entry. **Origin country:** China.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 150/210 m/s; armament: 4 air-to-air missiles, no cannon in the proposed fit; handling 3/5, stable fast passes with broad close-range turns.
- **Gameplay fit:** Creates a missile-focused counterpoint to the F-35A's guided-strike role without inventing radar ranges or classified performance.

Any use of existing stealth rules is an arcade balance proposal, not a real detection-performance claim.

### 19. Mikoyan MiG-31BM

- **NATO reporting name:** Foxhound, for the MiG-31 family. **Role:** Long-range interceptor. **Era:** 2000s upgraded variant of a 1980s-service aircraft. **Origin country:** Russia for the upgrade; Soviet Union for the original MiG-31.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 170/235 m/s; armament: cannon and 4 air-to-air missiles within existing targeting limits; handling 2/5, slow roll, wide turns and strong straight-line acceleration.
- **Gameplay fit:** Intercepts strike aircraft quickly but needs support against agile dogfighters.

### 20. PAC/CAC JF-17 Thunder Block III

- **NATO reporting name:** None asserted; Thunder is a program name. **Role:** Lightweight multirole fighter. **Era:** 2020s Block III service entry; family entered service in the 2000s. **Origin countries:** Pakistan and China.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 135/190 m/s; armament: cannon, 2 air-to-air missiles and 2 guided-strike rounds; handling 4/5, forgiving medium-speed control with modest acceleration.
- **Gameplay fit:** Adds a balanced light fighter without claiming Pakistan belongs to a modern Eastern Bloc.

### 21. Sukhoi Su-34

- **NATO reporting name:** Fullback. **Role:** Tactical bomber and strike aircraft. **Era:** 2010s formal service entry. **Origin country:** Russia.
- **Suggested stats:** Speed 140/195 m/s; armament: cannon, 2 air-to-air missiles and 6 guided-strike rounds; handling 2/5, stable attack runs with slow roll and wide recovery turns.
- **Gameplay fit:** Provides a heavy tactical strike platform that benefits from escort and coordinated approaches.

### 22. Tupolev Tu-160

- **Gameplay fit:** Creates a large payload specialist rather than guaranteeing symmetric one-to-one faction matchups.

### 23. Sukhoi Su-25SM

- **NATO reporting name:** Frogfoot, for the Su-25 family. **Role:** Close-air-support attack aircraft. **Era:** 2000s SM upgrade of a 1980s-service aircraft. **Origin country:** Russia for the upgrade; Soviet Union for the original Su-25.

### 24. Yakovlev Yak-130

### 25. Mil Mi-28N

## Mode rules and acceptance boundaries

Roster count parity is not balance evidence. Start with bounded objective scenarios and test payload regeneration, missile reach, escort concentration, small-boat survivability and respawn distances before promising fair team matches. The trainer slots belong to training/race scenarios, not compulsory combat picks. Fleet units initially remain AI objectives; there is no claim that all 30 entries are player-selectable.

There is no carrier air wing in this starting roster. Carrier launch/recovery would need a separately approved aircraft-variant roster and implementation contract; do not silently convert F-35A to F-35B/C, Rafale C to M, or J-20 to a naval fighter. Faction labels do not change existing solo/offline guarantees or authorize accounts, matchmaking, signaling servers or other backend work.
