### Concrete loop and degree of fit

Gather food/wood/stone, build housing and storage, recruit population, assign farmers/woodcutters/miners and specialist jobs, fund military/fortifications, then attack increasingly large simulated settlements. Workers continuously produce resources rather than collectible characters producing currency. Home attacks consume defenses and can kill workers or steal resources; preserving production while deploying military is the tradeoff. Source `onInvade()` prepares enemy population/fortifications and randomized loot; `doRaid()` determines victory or defeat; `plunder()` collects the reward and unlocks larger targets. `doMobs()` already provides local opponents. No multiplayer replacement or new network protocol is needed.

Progress is population/resources, upgrades, achievements and wonders, not an invented final victory screen. Raids have concrete win/loss outcomes. Starvation kills population; failed raids lose units; recovery is gathering food, rebuilding workforce and retraining. Existing reset retains prestige-related progression; Delete Save is a separate confirmed destructive action. Preserve that distinction. This is a complete incremental game source, not an engine demo, but lacks stealing individual income-producing collectibles and recovering those same stolen units. Do not claim otherwise or add that major new subsystem under a wrapper task.

Current controls are pointer-operated DOM buttons, resource quantities and settings. Numerous inline `onmousedown` handlers mean keyboard activation must be tested and repaired, not assumed accessible because elements are buttons. The module does expose actions on `window`, so the mere presence of inline handlers is not evidence of a broken launch. Proposed input is native button click/Enter/Space, Tab navigation and finger taps; no joystick or camera needed.
