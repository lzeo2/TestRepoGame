## Gameplay and controls

Seven prompts per run, 25 seconds each, Enter/Lock answer or Pass. Six editorial tiers award 10/15/30/60/85/100 points, ten metres per point, maximum 700 / 7,000 m. Exact normalized canonical names/aliases take precedence. Only an unrecognized answer receives a current-prompt-only full Damerau-Levenshtein pass. A unique nearest canonical answer within two edits is accepted one tier lower (floor tier 0 / 10 points), with an inline close-spelling note. Distances at least half the normalized input's code-point length, inputs/labels shorter than four code points, digit/symbol spellings, prefix completions and equally near different answers are rejected. Multiple aliases for one answer do not vote; group order does not break ties. NFKD/accent normalization happens before both passes.

Accepted canonical answers become used across prompts and replays of that seed/day, including aliases and corrected typos. Already-used submission shows 'Already answered. Try another answer.' without consuming a round, awarding points or resetting the clock. Timeout takes precedence over duplicate checks. Unknown/pass/timeout still consume the round for zero. Reveal retains the answer key and adds a reef-median comparison. Each prompt's `medianLampreyScore` is the median of its canonical-answer tier point values, ignoring aliases; even counts average the middle two. Constants are 45 except amino acids (30), validated against data at startup. They are fixed pseudo-scores, not opponents or observed player data. The final comparison uses the sum of the seven prompt constants. The existing total/best-find receipt, first-on-tie rule, replay/home and keyboard/touch/focus paths remain. Dive log is available from launch and results; its heading receives focus.

## State and persistence

`DiveCore` owns finite-clock create/submit/expire/next and frozen round records. `performance.now()` and one interval retain existing deadline/pagehide/pageshow/visibility behavior. Active runs are not restored. UTC daily selection still hashes only dataset version/mode/key; medians do not change the version or selected prompts.

`dive-trivia:history:v2` stores `{seeds:[{id,answers}],results:[{mode,key,date,total,tiers}]}`. Seed ID is JSON `[datasetVersion,mode,key,date]`; daily date is its UTC key, while pack/numbered runs capture the UTC launch date. Accepted normalized canonical identities save immediately, even for subsequently abandoned runs. Same-day replay shares them; another date/version/mode/key does not. Shared aliases for different canonical answers in different prompts remain distinct (for example W for Tungsten versus Watt). Results append only after all seven rounds finish. Parsing reconstructs validated fields, rejecting corrupt dates, inconsistent tier/score totals and malformed entries. Read/write/security/quota failures are nonfatal; the live page keeps its in-memory history and warns that persistence failed. No storage or cross-device anti-cheat guarantee is made.

Dive log shows best depth across all completed modes, total completed dives, tier counts across all completions, and best completed daily per UTC day. Streak counts consecutive completed daily dates ending today or yesterday (today remains available); zero-score completion counts, replays cannot add another streak day, and a missed UTC day breaks it. No active/abandoned result enters the chart or completion count. History grows until browser quota; there is no silent truncation, server sync or legacy-score migration. Existing `dive-trivia:color-theme` and `dive-trivia:last:v1` retain their guarded preference/last-result formats. Missing/blocked theme storage defaults to dark; the legacy glow key remains ignored. Sonar remains opt-in, session-only local WebAudio. The guide's 850ms score transition respects reduced motion. No accounts, analytics, network transports or LLM APIs.
