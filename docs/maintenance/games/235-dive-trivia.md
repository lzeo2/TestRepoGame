## Gameplay and controls

Seven prompts per run, 25 seconds each, type any recognized answer then Enter/Lock answer, or Pass. Six editorial tiers award 10/15/30/60/85/100 points, ten metres per point. Scores accumulate to at most 700 / 7,000 m. Timeout, unknown and pass score zero without ending the run. Reveal includes the finite answer key. Next starts a fresh question; round seven has a compact receipt (total depth and best recognized find), replay and home. Best find is the highest-point answer; ties retain the first. No per-round history is rendered in the final receipt. Quit confirms loss of unfinished progress. Keyboard, touch/native keyboard, mouse, theme and visible focus are supported. No invented lose condition for an open score challenge.

## State and persistence

`DiveCore` supplies finite-clock create/submit/expire/next operations. Submit is locked once; receipt at the deadline is too late; records freeze. Controller uses `performance.now()` and one interval, cleared at reveal/home/pagehide; pageshow restores a live timer and visibility return rechecks expiry. No active run is restored after reload. UTC daily seed/version selects everyone’s same seven, independent of timezone and local storage. Packs and numbered 1–999999 dives are deterministic. Keys `dive-trivia:theme` and `dive-trivia:last:v1` store glow preference and only one completed `{label,total}` summary. The legacy theme values remain compatible: `light` now means normal glow, `dark` means dim glow (`body.dark`), both on the same abyss background. The toggle is labeled Dim glow / Restore glow; there is no cream theme. Depth readout includes an original depth-dependent flavour line. Storage reads/parsing/bounds/writes are guarded; blocked saving reports nonfatal feedback. Sonar is session-only and off on each reload; user activation creates/resumes WebAudio, each positive score plays one quiet 240ms sine blip. Toggle off suspends it; unsupported audio reports unavailable. Guide position is total/700 of the ruler, eased over 850ms, with a separate 2s bob. Reduced motion disables both. No accounts or analytics.
