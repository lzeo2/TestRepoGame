## Gameplay and controls

Choose Cindupp, Dewgob or Pithnip, optionally enter integer seed 0–4294967295, then Start new expedition. The team begins with one level-three ally, eight kites, 90 Marks, two patches and two charges. Explore five regions: Rootfold Meadow, Stillwater Reach, Emberstep Quarry, Stonefold Ridge, Quietfold Hollow. Trials against Maren/Sola/Neri/Iven/Oren unlock sequential travel. After five credited trials, complete three explicit return lessons at existing camps in regions 0, 1, 4 with Maren, Sola and Oren. Preview fixed opponents, then Begin or Back; normal travel and free recovery remain. The final win persists campaign completion and offers Continue free play; all five original trial sites then offer explicit rematches. No new maps/species or terminal bonus payout. Collection remains a separate 80-species goal.

Focus canvas or main controls for WASD/arrows. Movement is camera-relative, normalized, four units/second and constrained by authored obstacles. Click/tap a trail marker or nearby button to route toward it; E/Interact activates within 1.8 units. Held Forward/Left/Back/Right controls are generated under `world-help`, with pointer capture and cancellation handling. Drag orbit and R/Reset camera are evidenced in controller/view. Touch world orbit requires multiple pointers after movement; a single touch tap selects a marker. Do not describe this as unrestricted free-flight or per-limb character animation.

Camp restores all roster HP/energy and at least four kites without Marks cost. Battle uses one active member of up to three allies, four abilities on 1–4, C for capture and Space for Wait when canvas/main has game focus. Buttons provide touch equivalents; switches and Flee are explicit buttons. Capture is only living wild creatures at <=half maximum HP. Each eligible attempt spends one kite; failure consumes a turn. Rival allies cannot be captured or fled from. Wait restores three energy plus trait/class bonuses. Damage uses attack/defense and element wheel: strong 1.5, reverse 0.75, otherwise 1. Shield/status/modifier expiration follows owner turns. Hush adds two energy cost; Scorch does six shield-bypassing HP damage.

Victory/capture awards XP and Marks; `gainXP` uses 30 + 12*current level thresholds, caps level 40, and follows explicit evolution levels 12/26. Level gains add resource-max deltas, not full healing. Defeat returns to camp with collection/progress preserved. `result-continue` restores exploration. Reset/new expedition requires confirmation before replacing prior progress. Pause P/Escape stops controller time; dialogs and hidden documents also stop simulation. No audio module, music or sound controls are present.

Ledger filters name/family and element. It edits team/favorites/accessories and offers confirmed release by individual UID. Guards preserve the last ally, team members, favorites, last conscious ally and pending battle. Services offer kit recovery, stock trades, cosmetic ownership, class selection and appearance editing, with field/outpost/shop/contract requirements enforced in shared `serviceAction`. Classes now have history-derived ranks 0..3; 0 means locked. Rank-2/3 thresholds are Pathfinder 3/9 claimed supplies, Binder 10/25 caught species, Warden 3/5 trials, Tactician 4/5 caught elements, Quartermaster 3/6 contracts. Rank-1/2/3 benefits are extra fiber 1/2/3, capture .04/.06/.08, shield 2/3/4, incoming switch energy 1/2/3 and delivery Marks 5/7/10 respectively. Transactions use the preceding rank; new fights pin earned ranks, old pending fights retain baseline ranks. Human player/NPC primitives are static multipart shapes with whole-body bobbing; there is no skeleton or per-limb animation in `human`/`render`.

## State and persistence

`script.js` owns `state`, `battle`, `phase`, `paused`, `busy`, route and dialog/inspection bookkeeping. Phases are `menu`, `world`, `dialogue`, `battle`, `result`; view modes separately are `world`, `battle`, `inspection`. `command` calls pure `applyAction`, copies canonical party resources, finishes once via `battleFinished`, saves and requests a visual effect. Challenge terminal commands instead pass the immediate pre-command checkpoint to `settleChallenge` BEFORE resource copying; that pure helper proves the ended model matches one legal deterministic next action. It clears pending context, grants ordinary win rewards/one stage, or free loss recovery, and increments encounter once. Normal wild/trial settlement remains separate. Shared result presentation replaces stale command messages and directs keyboard focus to the enabled Continue button. A 0.45-second controller busy window prevents rapid commands.

Storage keys in `world.js` are **`foldwild-save-v1`** and **`foldwild-save-backup-v1`**, even though canonical save version is 3. Do not rename keys to match version number. `validateSave` accepts versions 1/2/3, projects canonical fields and migrates legacy location/progression/build/economy defaults. V3 requires `finaleStage` 0..3 and exact nullable `pendingChallenge` context; old versions migrate unstarted/null, never implicit completion, and cannot smuggle nondefault progress/ranks. Context requires exact location/stage/enemy/team/build identity and ongoing battle. Nonzero completion requires all five trials. Old runtimes reject v3 on reload; close/export historical tabs before upgrading because already-open noncooperating writers remain a disclosed limit. `legacyRivals` preserves the old third-trial badge without inventing a new five-trial completion. Seeds and encounter indices deterministically regenerate world selections independently from battle RNG.

Save boundary rejects malformed descriptors/getters, non-plain records, invalid IDs, UID duplication, illegal palettes/classes/economy, invalid active battle or inconsistent resources. Caps include 160 roster members, three team members, 256 KiB UTF-8 serialized saves and 10,000 pending rounds. `pendingBattleCopy` validates exact team correspondence, effects/active slots/RNG and generated enemy identity. Reload may resume the precise next command, not regenerate an encounter as a new fight.

`writeSave(state,storage,expectedPrimary)` validates and checks exact primary bytes before any write; conflict preserves both slots. `readSave(storage,true)` additionally returns exact raw metadata; corrupt or denied storage is not silently replaced. The controller captures canonical snapshots, queues writes, and uses exclusive native Web Locks for cooperating tabs when available. Without locks it is explicitly optimistic, not atomic. Movement enqueues once dirty and at least one active second elapsed; other actions/preferences enqueue at their existing boundaries. Only actual completion reports success. Errors latch automatic saving off until explicit replacement/recovery; old/noncooperating writers and ABA changes are not fully protected.

The always-visible `save-tools` area separates `save-state`, `render-state` and coordination disclosure from action messages. It provides validated JSON Blob export, a local file input capped at 256 KiB before reading, explicit backup preview, exact-primary-byte replacement consent and reload warning. Preview waits for the current queue; confirmation still refuses a subsequently changed primary. Recovery is explicit, never automatic fallback. `beforeunload` only cancels RAF/disposes view: queued or unsaved work may be lost; wait for saved status before closing. Backup writes precede primary writes, so primary-only quota can rotate backup before failing; no two-key transaction is promised. A failed write leaves in-memory play available, not durably saved.

`foldwildSnapshot` is a getter deep-freezing a structured clone of phase/state/battle/view diagnostics. It is read-only and should not become a setter for native tests. `frame` owns sole clock/RAF, clamps simulation dt to 0.05, and renders at 30 Hz low or 60 Hz standard. It may render frozen time while a dialog is open, allowing inspector controls to update. Hidden documents skip view render. Low buffer is <=960x540, standard <=1280x720 at DPR 1; these are caps, not measured frame-rate guarantees.
