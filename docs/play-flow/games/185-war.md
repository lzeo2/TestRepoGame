## Flow

- Boot: static markup; `#startScreen` visible (h1 "War", rules text), `#playScreen`/`#endOverlay` hidden.
- Start/setup: `#startBtn` -> `startGame()`: new `Game`, players `['You','Computer']`, 52-card deck shuffled (Fisher-Yates), dealt round-robin while `cardsLeft.length >= players.length` (26 each, remainder stays in `deck.slice` copy - actually popped until fewer than 2 left; 52 is even so 26/26), `roundsWon` reset, focus `#actionBtn`.
- Input: `#actionBtn` click (label flips between "Flip" and "War") + Space when `e.target === document.body`; `#restartBtn`/`#playAgainBtn` -> `startGame`.
- Core loop: `flip()` -> `snapshotHands(game.war)` (captures the cards about to be played: last card, or `length-2` in war) -> `act()`: normal `playRound()` (both pop into `pot`, `findWinner(pot)`), or `playWar()` when `game.war`. Tie -> `war = true`, message "War! Equal cards. Press War to lay a card down and flip again.", button label "War". War rule from source: players with `< 2` cards sacrifice their remaining hand and are eliminated; if all are eliminated -> `game.draw = true`. Pot goes to `game.winner.hand.unshift(...pot)`; `removeLosers()` drops zero-hand players.
- Score/progression: `#scoreline` "You N cards, computer M cards. Rounds won: you X, computer Y."; `updateCounts` also fills `#yourCount`/`#cpuCount`.
- Win/lose: `showEnd()` when `players.length === 1` or `draw` - `#endOverlay` "You win the match"/"You lose the match"/"Draw" with exact counts in `#endText`.
- Restart/exit: `#playAgainBtn` and `#restartBtn` both `startGame`; no dead ends.
