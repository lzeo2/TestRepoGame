## Historical: detail/terrain iteration, 13 November 2026

Actual software WebGL captures of the detail/terrain/teleport iteration, with
[36 browser checks](../verification/capital-drive-2026-11-13.md). They show
original procedural models over real-source geography, not photogrammetry or
measured Intel N100 performance.

- [Telopea street-facing school](2026-11-13/telopea-school-facade.png):
  brick/cream treatment, framed windows, mature canopy and walking pedestrians.
- [Manuka streets](2026-11-13/manuka-streets.png): low shop forms, awnings and
  fuller retail frontages.
- [Kingston streets](2026-11-13/kingston-streets.png): denser source outlines
  and shopping-street treatment.
- [Red Hill](2026-11-13/redhill-streets.png): contour-derived slopes and
  residential streets.
- [Selectable teleport map](2026-11-13/map-teleport.png): real roads/outlines,
  zoom/pan and an arbitrary selected destination.

The school view uses normal mouse orbit; destinations use the ordinary map
controls. No gameplay setters, scene mocks or Google images were used for these
captures. See [the inspected street references](../capital-drive-street-references.md)
and [provenance](../capital-drive-sources.md) for actual observations and limits.
