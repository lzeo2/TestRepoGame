Boggle

Chain touching letters into words and beat the target score before the three-minute clock runs out.

  • Tap or click neighboring letters to build a chain (letters must touch, including diagonally, and cannot repeat).
  • Submit with the button, the S key, or Enter on the button. Backspace removes the last letter, Esc clears the chain.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move between cells, Enter adds or removes a letter.
  • Words score by length: 3 letters = 1 point, 4 = 2, 5 = 4, 6 = 7, 7 = 11.
  • Reach the target score to win. When time runs out, falling short loses the game.
