FreeCell

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one suit per pile, ace up to king. Eight cascades build down in alternating colors, and the four free cells each hold one card while you plan ahead. Tap a card, then tap where it should go; in Auto mode one tap sends a card to its best spot. Keyboard: Tab to a card and press Enter to move it, U undoes, A toggles Auto/Pick, N starts a new game.
