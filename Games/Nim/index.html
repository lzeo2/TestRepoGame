Nim

A single pile of stones. Take 1, 2 or 3 stones per turn. The computer answers in kind.

Whoever takes the last stone wins this mode.

Controls
  • Take stones: tap Take 1, Take 2 or Take 3, or press keys 1, 2, 3.
  • New round: press N or use the New round button.
  • Menu: the Menu button returns here to switch modes or reset the score.
  • Works with mouse, touch and keyboard. The running score is kept across rounds.
