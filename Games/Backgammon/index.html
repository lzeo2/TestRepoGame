Backgammon

Full backgammon against a simple computer opponent. Roll happens automatically each turn. You are white: move all fifteen checkers from point 24 down to your home board (points 1 to 6), hit the opponent's loose blots on the way, and bear off past point 1. The computer plays black upward toward 24. First to bear off all fifteen checkers wins the match.

Rules in play
  • Dice: two dice per turn, rolled automatically. Doubles play four moves of that number.
  • Legal moves only: destinations light up green. Both dice must be used when they can be.
  • Bar: landing on a lone enemy blot sends it to the bar. Checkers on the bar re-enter first.
  • Bearing off: once all your checkers are home, move off the board with an exact roll, or a higher roll when no checker sits farther out. An exact roll is needed while a checker remains farther out.
  • Score: 5 points per blot hit, 2 per checker borne off, 100 for winning the match. Record shows matches won and lost.
Controls
  • Touch / mouse: tap one of your checkers, then tap a glowing destination point. Tap the glowing Off tray to bear off. Tap the selected checker again to deselect.
  • Keyboard: Tab or Shift+Tab moves between points, bar and Off tray; Enter or Space selects the focused square and confirms a move. Buttons in the header start a new match or return to the menu.
  • Board map: the grid snakes in four-point rows: points 24 to 21, then 17 to 20, 16 to 13, the bar, then 9 to 12, 8 to 5, and 1 to 4. Point numbers are printed on each point.
