Driving controls

W / Up: gas. S / Down: brake, then reverse in City and Sandbox; brake only on highways. A / D or Left / Right: steer. P / Escape: pause. C / camera button: switch chase and cockpit views. M / Map: toggle the north-up minimap. Space / E: deploy a mounted kit outside Sprint. On phones, hold Gas or Reverse (brakes first). Auto gas is optional. Sandbox offers every car and visit-only steering strength/response controls without saving career progress.

City: explore, then Garage parks and banks distance. Patrol starts after 20 active seconds and 30 meters; stay 75 meters away for 6 seconds to escape. Buildings, traffic and police can damage your car. A crash notice shows actual body damage; its Respawn button abandons the unfinished drive unpaid. A wreck or arrest shows a three-second countdown, then respawns you in the same mode with auto gas off.

Cutup: reach the highway endpoint while avoiding traffic and police. Sprint: beat three rivals. Returning to the garage during either highway mode abandons it unpaid. Reloading an unfinished drive also pays nothing.

Banked mileage unlocks cars; cash buys performance upgrades and eligible kits. Paint, wheels, stripe and spoiler are free. Smoke and decoy disrupt pursuit; EMP and boost raise heat; repair restores bodywork. Charges reset each drive. Hidden tabs pause play.
