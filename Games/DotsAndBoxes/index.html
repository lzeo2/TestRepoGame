Dots and Boxes

Claim the lines between the dots. Complete a box and you score points and go again. Fill the board to win.

Controls
  • Claim a line: tap or click it, or move the keyboard cursor with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space.
  • New round: press N or use the New round button.
  • Menu: the Menu button returns here to switch modes.
  • 16 boxes per round, 10 points each. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard.
