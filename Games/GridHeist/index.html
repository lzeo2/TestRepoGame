Grid Heist

Race for the vault at the centre of the grid. On your turn you claim one cell next to your territory. First to reach the vault captures it — and every cell of yours that stays connected to it scores double. Block your rivals' routes!

Keyboard: 2 / 3 / 4 to pick players · Enter to start · arrows / WASD to move · Space to claim
