Maintenance bay / training system

Circuit Ward

Hold the bay through six waves. Disable walkers and hover drones, use four consoles for cover, and collect repair cells for +25 shield.

Controls

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys. Aim: mouse or IJKL.
  • Fire: click or hold Space. Unlimited pulses.
  • Pause: P or Esc. Resume with the button, then click the bay to capture the mouse.
  • Touch: left pad to move, drag the right pad to aim, hold Fire.

Walkers: 100 points. Drones: 150. Co-op pools score; each player keeps their own shield. Disabled teammates return after a wave is cleared.

Original, operator-approved game. Built with locally vendored three.js (MIT).
