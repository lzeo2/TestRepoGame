Help

Pull the character at the sling back and release. A fresh click or tap on the court works once during flight; the ability button or Space is an alternative: Jack dashes, Ian braces, Sim hops.

With the court focused, Left/Right changes angle, Up/Down changes pull, and Enter or Space launches. P pauses. Escape cancels a pull. Aim contains labelled keyboard alternatives.

Clear fictional teachers by actual character contact, moving blocks or a fall to the ground. Retry resets the current level. Map shows twenty authored drafts, unlocked in order. Earned stars and best scores save locally. Skins only change colors: earn stars or clear level 20. Session only means storage is blocked.
