Curve Fever

Your line keeps moving and keeps growing. Steer it, leave no gap behind, and outlive the other player. Crashing into any line or the border ends your round. First to 5 round wins takes the match.

Controls, 1P vs computer: Arrow keys or A and D steer your line, or hold the left and right steer buttons. Controls, 2P on one keyboard: Player 1 uses A and D, Player 2 uses the left and right Arrow keys. Touch: use the steer button pairs below the arena.
