Archery

Controls: press on the field near the bow and drag toward the target, then release to shoot (mouse or touch); the farther you drag, the stronger the draw. Keyboard: Left/Right aim, Up/Down draw power, Space release, R restart. Ten arrows, score 15 points to win. Rings score 1, 2, 3 and 4 points (bull = 4); wind is re-rolled every shot and pushes each arrow in flight.
