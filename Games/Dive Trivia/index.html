How rarity and answers work

Rarity is editor-assigned, not measured from players. Every point takes you 10 metres deeper. Capitalization, accents and listed spelling variants are accepted. There is no spelling guesser.

The answer key is curated and finite. An unlisted answer may still be valid, but scores zero here. One submission per prompt, with a review before the next question. Replays are practice, not a global competition.

Daily prompts use the UTC date and dataset version. The same version and date give everyone the same seven prompts. Packs and numbered dives can repeat daily questions.
