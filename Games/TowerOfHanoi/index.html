Tower of Hanoi

Move the whole stack from the first peg to another peg. Only the top disk of a peg may move, and a larger disk never goes on a smaller one.

You get exactly the minimal budget of 2^n - 1 moves. Spend it well: running out ends the round. Undo is free.

Controls: tap a peg to pick up its top disk, then tap another peg to drop it. Keyboard: 1, 2 and 3 select pegs, U undoes, R resets. Choose your disk count to start.
