Hangman Rush

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before the gallows are complete.

  • Each puzzle belongs to a category shown on screen. Words are from the built-in list only.
  • Reveal every letter to win the puzzle. Each miss draws one more part of the hangman.
  • You get 7 guesses per puzzle. Lose all of them and the run ends.
  • A solved puzzle is worth 100 points plus 25 for every spare guess left.
