Jacks or Better

Draw five cards, hold the ones you like and draw again. Pairs of jacks or better pay back your bet. Start with 1,000 chips; doubling that wins the session. Tap a card to hold or release it, then press Deal to draw, and use the plus and minus buttons to change the bet. Keyboard: 1 to 5 holds a card, Enter or Space deals, Arrow Up and Arrow Down change the bet.
