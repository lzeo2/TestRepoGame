Solo controls

Bot matches: random maps, scoring, round ends and automatic replay. Tab or T opens match reset and mode voting. Sandbox: seed 1 by default, passive targets, unlimited ammunition; B toggles live bots. No server needed.

WASD or arrows: move. Mouse: aim. Left click: fire. Right click: look ahead. Shift: sprint. G: equipment. 1/2/3: weapon. 4/5: equipment. Tab or T: scores. M: mute.

Touch: arrows to move, drag on the scene to aim, Fire to shoot.
