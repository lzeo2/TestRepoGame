Klondike Solitaire

Classic draw-one Klondike. Build the four foundations up from ace to king, alternating colors on the tableau.

Controls
  • Move a card: click or tap it and it jumps to the best spot, or drag it with the mouse.
  • To the foundation: double-click, or press Shift+Enter on the focused card.
  • Draw from the stock: click the stock or press S. An empty stock recycles the waste.
  • Keyboard: Tab between cards and buttons, Enter moves a card, U undo, N new game.
  • Goal: all 52 cards to the foundations. Run out of legal moves and the round is lost.
