Curling

Three ends against the AI, two stones per side each end. Slide your stones into the house: the team whose stone lies closest to the button scores one point for every stone counting in front of the rival's best.

Touch: drag on the ice to aim, hold Slide to set weight, release to throw, hold Sweep while your stone travels. Keyboard: Left and Right aim, hold Space for weight, release to throw, hold S to sweep. R restarts.
