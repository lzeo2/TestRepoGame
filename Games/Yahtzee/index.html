Yahtzee

Thirteen rounds, five dice, one scorecard. Roll up to three times per round, hold the dice you want to keep, then bank a score in any open row. Six or more ones in the upper section earn a 35 point bonus. Reach 200 points to win the session.

Controls: tap a die to hold or release it, tap Roll, then tap a scorecard row to score it. Keyboard: Space rolls, 1 to 5 toggles a held die, Tab moves through the scorecard and Enter scores the highlighted row.
