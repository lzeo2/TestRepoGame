Free Throw

Hold the Shoot button (or Space) to run the power meter, release when the bar is inside the green zone, and sink at least 12 points from 10 shots to win.

Controls: Left and Right arrows (or the arrow buttons) aim. Hold and release Space or Shoot to set power. Press R to restart.

Round over

Final score: 0 of 20 possible.

Controls: hold Space or the Shoot button to charge power, release in the green band (about 70 percent) for an accurate shot. Left and Right arrows aim. R restarts the round. Score 12 or more from 10 shots to win. Rim bounces can drop or spare a shot.
