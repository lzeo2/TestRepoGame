Higher Or Lower

One real fifty-two card deck, no jokers, shuffled each round. You are shown a card; guess whether the next card drawn is higher or lower. Guessing right grows your streak and your score. Reach a streak of eight to win the round. One wrong guess and the round is over.

Rules
  • Card order: 2 is low, Ace is high. Suits do not matter.
  • Ties lose: if the next card has the same rank, the round is lost.
  • Score: each correct guess pays 10 times your current streak. Best streak is tracked for the session.
  • Cards left: the deck shrinks with every draw, so remember what has already been played.
Controls
  • Touch / mouse: tap the Higher or Lower button.
  • Keyboard: H or the Up arrow guesses higher, L or the Down arrow guesses lower.
