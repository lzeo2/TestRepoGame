Gladihoppers

2d physics gladiator brawler with career mode, gear upgrades, and 2-player local fights.

  • W, A, S, D or the arrow keys: move your gladiator
  • Pick each fighter’s bindings (WASD, arrows, or gamepad) in the in-game pre-fight menu
  • Mouse or touch: menus and targeting, with an in-game tutorial for stances and attacks
  • 2-player local: both fighters share one keyboard, or use a gamepad for one side
