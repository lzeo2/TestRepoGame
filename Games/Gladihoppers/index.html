Gladihoppers

2D physics gladiator brawler — career mode, gear upgrades, 2-player local fights.

  • W A S D / ↑ ↓ ← → — move your gladiator
  • Pick each fighter’s bindings (WASD, Arrows, or gamepad) in the in-game pre-fight menu
  • Mouse / touch — menus and targeting; in-game tutorial covers stances & attacks
  • 2-player local: both fighters share one keyboard (or a gamepad for one side)
