Mahjong Lite

Classic tile-matching solitaire on the turtle layout: 144 tiles stacked in four layers. Tap a free tile to select it, then tap a matching free tile to remove the pair; a tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and its left or right side is open. Arrow keys move between free tiles, Enter selects. Score 10 per pair plus a time bonus; hints cost 5 points. Clear all 144 tiles to win; if no pairs remain, the game is lost.
