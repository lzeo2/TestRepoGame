Duck Hunt

Three rounds of rising ducks. Each round gives you a small stack of shots and a quota to knock down before the ducks get away.

Controls: move the crosshair with the mouse, then click to fire. On touch, tap the spot you want to shoot. Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD move the crosshair, Space fires.
