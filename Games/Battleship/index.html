Battleship

Sink all 10 computer ships before it sinks yours. Click or tap a cell on the computer's grid to fire. With the keyboard, move the aim with the arrow keys and press Enter to fire. A hit lets you fire again. Press Restart Game whenever you want a new match.

Rules

Each side has 10 ships on a 10 by 10 grid: 1 battleship of 4 cells, 2 cruisers of 3 cells, 3 destroyers of 2 cells and 4 submarines of 1 cell. Ships never touch each other, not even diagonally.

Take turns firing at one cell at a time. If you hit, you fire again. If you miss, the computer fires. When a ship is sunk, the empty cells around it are revealed. Sink every enemy ship to win.
