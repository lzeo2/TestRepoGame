Mini Golf

Six holes, obstacles and walls, stroke counter against par. Match or beat par (19 strokes) to win the round.

Touch: press and drag back from anywhere to aim, release to putt. Keyboard: Left and Right rotate the aim line, hold Space to charge, release to putt. R restarts the round.

Holed

Round over

Controls: drag back from the ball and release to putt (mouse or touch), or Left/Right to rotate the aim line, hold Space (or the Slide button) to charge and release to putt. Bumpers bounce the ball, sand slows it, walls bank it. Sink all six holes in 19 strokes or fewer to win. R restarts.
