Checkers

Capture every rival piece or leave the computer with no legal move. Jumps are forced: when a capture is available you must take it. Men move forward only; reaching the far row crowns a king that moves both ways. You play the light pieces, moving up; the computer plays dark. Tap a piece, then tap the square to move or jump, or use the arrow keys to move the cursor and Enter to pick up or drop a piece.
