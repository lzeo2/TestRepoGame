Dominoes

How scoring works: play matching tiles onto the chain until one player is out; the winner scores the opponent's remaining pips. If the boneyard runs out and both players are stuck, the round goes to the player with fewer pips (the difference is scored; ties score nothing). First to 100 points wins the match.

Controls: tap a tile to play it. When a tile fits both ends, pick Left or Right. When you have no play, tap Draw to take a tile from the boneyard. Keyboard: Tab moves through tiles and buttons, Enter activates the focused one.
