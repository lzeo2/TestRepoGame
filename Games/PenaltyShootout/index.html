Penalty Shootout

Best of 5 against the AI. Aim with the arrows (three heights), hold the charge button or Space to set power, release near the green band. The AI keeper reads your aim half the time. When the AI kicks, you go in goal.

Controls: Left/Right/Up/Down aim, hold and release Space (or the charge button) to shoot. A, S, D choose your dive in goal. R restarts.

Shootout over

Controls: Left/Right move the aim across the goal, Up/Down pick low, middle or high. Hold Space (or the charge button) to build power, release to kick; the green band is the sweet spot, power under 12 is too weak and high power scatters the shot. On defense press A, S or D to dive. R restarts.
