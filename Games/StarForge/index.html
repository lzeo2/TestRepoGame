Star Forge

A dying star lattice must be forged into star metal before the reactor melts down. Tap the cores hollow before the clock hits zero.

How to play

  • Four star cores each hold 250 integrity. Every hit removes 1. Forge all 1000 hits out to win.
  • Extractor drones hit cores for you, but every drone tap burns energy. Energy refills slowly; buy Vents to refill it faster.
  • Hire more Drones to hit harder, but watch the energy bar: if it empties, your drones stall and the clock keeps running.
  • Win: all four cores reach 0 before 120 seconds pass.
  • Lose: the meltdown timer reaches 0 with any core still holding.

Controls

  • Tap / click a star core to strike it directly (free, no energy cost).
  • Keys 1 to 4: strike core 1, 2, 3 or 4.
  • Space: strike the core with the most integrity left.
  • D: hire a drone. V: buy a vent.
  • R: forge again after a round ends.
