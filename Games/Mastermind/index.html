Mastermind

Break the hidden code. Each round the computer picks a secret row of 4 colors from 6. Colors can repeat. You have 8 guesses.

How to read the feedback pegs
  • Gold peg: right color, right spot.
  • White peg: right color, wrong spot.
  • Pegs do not say which color matched, and duplicates count.
Controls
  • Mouse / touch: click a hole in the current row, pick a color in the popup, fill all 4 holes, then click the check mark at the left of the row.
  • Keyboard: Left / Right move the cursor, keys 1 to 6 place a color, Enter submits a full row, Esc closes the color popup.
  • New round starts a fresh code, Give up reveals the code. The score carries across rounds.
