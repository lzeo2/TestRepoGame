Hunt The Wumpus

You are hunting the Wumpus in a cave of twenty rooms joined by tunnels. Each room has three tunnels. Two rooms hide bottomless pits, two hide super bats, and one hides the Wumpus. You carry five arrows.

Rules
  • Smell, draft, flapping: the Wumpus, a pit or the bats are in a room next to yours.
  • Move into a pit and you fall. Step into the Wumpus and it eats you. Bats carry you to a random safe-from-bats room.
  • Shoot an arrow through up to three tunnels. Hit the Wumpus to win. An arrow that loops back to your room hits you. Running out of arrows loses the hunt.
  • Score: 100 points per win plus 20 for every arrow left. Wins in a row build your streak.
Controls
  • Touch / mouse: tap a tunnel button to move, or tap Shoot arrow, pick up to three tunnels, then Fire. Cancel aborts the shot.
  • Keyboard: keys 1 2 3 pick the tunnels in order (move or extend the shot), S starts shooting, Enter fires, Esc cancels, M returns to move mode.
