Stockfish 17.1 Lite

Chess engine by the Stockfish developers. The single-threaded WebAssembly port is by Nathan Rugg and Chess.com, with the Lite network by Linmiao Xu.

Stockfish searches run on this device. Bot Elo values describe strength targets. Below 1320, the bot mixes in random legal moves.
