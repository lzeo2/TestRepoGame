Word Ladder

Change one letter at a time and climb from the start word to the target word.

  • Type a four-letter word from the built-in list that differs from the current top word by exactly one letter.
  • Press Submit or Enter to play it. Undo removes your last step.
  • Reach the target within 12 moves to win. Running out of moves loses the game.
  • Fewer moves than the par shown on screen is a better score.
