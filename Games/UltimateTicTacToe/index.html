Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

Nine little boards inside one big board. The square you play on sends your opponent to the matching board. Win three little boards in a row to take the game.

Forced cell. The first move can be anywhere. After that you must play in the mini-board matching the square your opponent just picked. If you are sent to a board that is already finished, you may play anywhere.

Claim boards. Get three in a row on a mini-board and you capture it. A captured board cannot be played again.

Win the game. Capture three mini-boards in a row, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, and you win. If every board fills up, the game is a draw.

Controls
  • Move the cursor: arrow keys across the full 9 by 9 grid.
  • Place your mark: Enter or Space, or tap / click a square.
  • New round: the Restart button or the Play again button in the end dialog.
  • Menu: returns here and resets the score. Esc closes the end dialog.
