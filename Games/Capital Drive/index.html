Guided controls

A short playable guide to walking, driving, routes, shops and jobs.

Move, drive and look
WASD / arrows
Walk or drive. S brakes, then reverses.
E
Stop a moving car; press again to step out. Beside your car, press E to get in.
Shift / Space
Sprint / jump on foot. Boost / handbrake in a car.
Mouse drag
Orbit the camera. Right mouse aims on foot.
Touch joysticks
Left stick moves or drives. Push up to accelerate, down to brake or reverse. Right stick looks around. Hold RUN or BOOST for speed; tap USE to interact.
Find places and transport
M · Map
Search a place, choose it and set a route. Follow the yellow arrow and distance.
T · Phone
Route to your car, request or track Yoober, and find Lemon rides.
Bus / Yoober
On foot, press E beside a stopped door to board. E requests a stop or exits when stopped.
Airport jets
Find a jet stand on the map. On foot, use E and complete the sequence to take one. W/S changes throttle, A/D turns, Space climbs and Shift descends. On touch use the left stick, CLIMB and DESCEND. Land and stop before E lets you out.
Jobs, shops and interiors
J · Jobs / Campaign
Read the objective and start a job or mission. Campaign lets you switch characters between missions.
K · Shops
Set a route, visit on foot, then enter and approach the counter. Buttons explain when a purchase is unavailable.
E / Q
Enter at a door; E leaves at the same door. Q starts the nearby activity. Follow its on-screen cues.
Gym bench
Hold X + Y. Press ↑ only when LIFT appears. Release only when the cue says to.
Combat and police
1–5 / R
Equip fists or an owned weapon / reload. Buy weapons and thrown equipment from supplies or street contacts using K.
Z / mouse / F
Hold Z or right mouse to aim, then move the mouse up/down to pitch. Page Up/Down also changes pitch. Left mouse fires; F punches. Tutorial and protected jobs disable attacks.
V / C
Throw a grenade / smoke grenade. Smoke blocks sight; grenades can hurt you. B throws a flare.
L · Power
Kit: faster movement and jumping. Rowan: faster shooting and reloads. Milo: more engine power and grip while driving. Each lasts 8 seconds and recharges in 40.
I · Recon camera
At a recon objective, frame the marked subject on foot and hold still. Press I or SCAN only when ready. The phone also opens the camera.
Evidence case
X/Y turns the dial. Match each displayed channel value, release the keys and press ↑ to confirm.
Pink Earmuffs
F punches. Step back during her windup and counter. Win by knocking Dot down after the noodle throw. Rematch retries without restoring your health.
Campaign crew
Living allies board free car seats while stopped and fire at threats through the passenger windows. On safe shared stages, open Campaign and choose a nearby ally while on foot.
Wanted stars
Break line of sight and leave the search area. Ordinary jobs remain available while wanted.
Recovery
Treatment and arrest cost cash and keep completed missions. Medical recovery returns you to a nearby safe road. Failed campaign missions retry automatically when recovery finishes; cancelling a mission stops retries.
Modes and performance
Street Life
Work, buy cars and explore with police and a shared wallet.
Campaign
Three characters with separate locations and a connected story.
Free roam
Try vehicles, search addresses and teleport on the map. Practice does not earn career progress.
Rendering
Pause → Performance reduces resolution and targets 20 FPS. Low targets 30; Standard targets 60. Actual speed depends on your device.
P / Esc · F1
Pause or close a menu / open this help.
