Controls & help

Guided controls

Practice the controls in game.

Move, drive and look
WASD / arrows
Walk or drive. S brakes, then reverses.
E
Stop a moving car; press again to get out. Press beside your car to get in.
Shift / Space
Shift sprints on foot or boosts while accelerating. Stop at a petrol bay and press E to refill nitro for $45. Space jumps on foot or starts a drift while turning at speed.
F · Car camera
While driving, switch between the cockpit and chase views. Drag to look around.
U · Jetpack
Buy at Canalside Supplies. U launches outside between jobs or requests landing. Space rises, Shift descends; release to hover. Fuel refills on the ground.
N · Headlights
Toggle headlights. They also come on at night.
Mouse drag
Orbit the camera. Right mouse aims on foot.
Touch joysticks
Left stick moves or drives; right stick looks. Hold RUN or BOOST for speed. USE interacts.
Find places and transport
M · Map
Search a place and set a route. Follow the yellow arrow.
T · Phone
Rides, deliveries, crew, safehouses and carried gear.
Bus / Yoober
On foot, press E beside a stopped door to board. E requests a stop or exits when stopped.
Airport jets
E at a stand starts the lock puzzle. Build runway speed with W, then press Space at the takeoff cue. Shift descends. Land and stop before E exits.
Swimming and boats
WASD swims; Shift speeds up. Buy at Boat Shed. W/S controls throttle, A/D steers and Space brakes. Stop before E exits.
Light rail
E calls a tram at a platform and boards when its doors open. Press E to request an exit, then again at the next platform.
Defence depot
Search the map for Capital Defence Depot. Press E beside the tank. Use WASD to drive and Space to brake.
Depot aircraft
Press E at the aircraft door and follow the lock cues. Use Space to climb. The interceptor needs a runway roll first.
Jobs, shops and interiors
J · Jobs / Campaign
Read the objective and start. Campaign characters switch between missions.
K · Shops
Visit on foot, enter and approach the counter. Unavailable purchases explain why.
E / Q
E enters or leaves through a door. Q starts a nearby activity; follow its cues.
Heists
Find Civic Mutual Bank or Manuka Facets. E enters. Aim at or punch the employee, then press E to demand loot. Collect it at the marker, leave and clear the search. The rail parcel job starts at its platform.
House break-ins
E at an unowned door starts its lock puzzle. Q searches furniture. Leave through the door to collect loot; noise can trigger an alarm.
Safehouses
Buy at the listed door using Phone → Safehouses. Store weapons, ammo and cash inside. The bed restores health for $80.
Gym bench
Hold X + Y. Press ↑ only when LIFT appears. Release only when the cue says to.
Combat and police
Weapon wheel / R
Hold Tab, or tap EQUIP on touch, to open the weapon wheel. Point or drag to a slot, then release or tap to equip. R reloads. Buy equipment using K. Hold aim for the sniper scope. Firing is paused while the wheel is open.
Z / mouse / F
Z or right mouse aims. Move the mouse or use Page Up/Down to adjust pitch. Left mouse fires; F punches. Tutorial and protected jobs disable attacks.
X · Crouch
Hold X or DUCK to move lower and more slowly. Use a wall or solid object between you and the threat for cover.
V / C
Throw a grenade or smoke grenade on foot. Smoke blocks sight; grenades can hurt you. B throws a flare.
L · Power
Kit moves and jumps faster; Rowan shoots and reloads faster; Milo gains engine power and grip. Effects last 8 seconds and recharge in 40.
I · Recon camera
At the marked objective, frame the subject and hold still. Press I or SCAN when ready. Also available in Phone.
Drone
Buy one from an equipment dealer, then launch from Phone while outside on foot. WASD moves, Space rises, Shift lowers; N toggles night vision, O recalls. Your body stays vulnerable.
Phone → Gear
Use medkits or repair kits, or press their hotkeys (8, 9 and 0 by default) without opening the phone. Rebind them under More. With a police scanner, Camera pulse marks nearby surveillance on both maps. A jammer temporarily disables nearby cameras.
Evidence case
X/Y turns the dial. Match each displayed channel value, release the keys and press ↑ to confirm.
Pink Earmuffs
F punches. Step back during her windup and counter. Win by knocking Dot down after the noodle throw. Rematch retries without restoring your health.
Campaign crew
Living allies follow indoors, board free car seats while stopped and defend the crew. At safe shared stages, Campaign lets you choose a nearby ally on foot.
Wanted stars
Break sight and leave the search area. Higher stars add SWAT, air searches and roadblocks. Ordinary jobs remain available.
Recovery
Medical recovery returns you to a safehouse with ammo. Arrest returns you to the station. Failed missions restore the pre-mission state; retry from Campaign.
Modes and performance
Street Life
Jobs, purchases and a saved career.
Campaign
Three characters and a connected story. Optional missions unlock after the main story.
Free roam
Practice with vehicles and equipment, address search and map travel. Career progress is unchanged.
Rendering
Pause offers four quality levels. Performance lowers resolution; Realism uses a sharper image. Frame rate depends on your device.
P / Esc · F1
Pause or close a menu / open this help.
