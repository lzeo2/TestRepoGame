Built around real Canberra.

Capital Drive is an original, single-player driving preview set in Canberra's inner south. The street network, building outlines and terrain come from the datasets below. All gameplay data and rendering dependencies are served locally.

Street network and OpenStreetMap outlines

© OpenStreetMap contributors. The local road and OSM outline database is available under the Open Database License 1.0. The original data was obtained through the official OpenStreetMap map API. The transformed snapshot is available as canberra.json, with source hashes and import details.

ACT building footprints

© Australian Capital Territory. ACTGOV_BUILDING_FOOTPRINTS, used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Footprints have been projected, simplified in presentation and supplemented with OSM outlines. The dataset is incomplete and does not establish current building appearance.

Terrain

Mapzen Terrain Tiles, obtained from the public Terrarium elevation tile archive. Australia terrain data © Commonwealth of Australia (Geoscience Australia) 2017. Global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. See the data notices and full terrain attribution. Elevations are sampled to a local grid and expressed relative to a 550-metre datum.

Rendering, vehicles and fonts

Three.js r160, © the three.js authors, MIT licence. Original fictional procedural vehicles reuse this repository's car-arcade model factory. Atkinson Hyperlegible by the Braille Institute, SIL Open Font License. Interface, driving rules and streetscape details are original work for this project.

What is approximate

Building heights, roof forms, facades, vegetation placement, parked vehicles and road furniture are visual interpretations. Pedestrians are fictional adults with simplified walking and car avoidance; they do not represent real people. The school is represented using real building outlines with approximate architectural details. Traffic uses simplified left-hand routing and spacing; decorative signals do not control traffic. This is not a Street View reconstruction, a current road-condition survey or a driving simulator.

Targets Intel N100 / 8 GB with low rendering settings. Actual target-device performance has not been measured. The first district is the inner south; other Canberra districts are not included yet.
