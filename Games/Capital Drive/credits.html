Built around real Canberra.

Capital Drive is an original, single-player exploration and action preview set in Canberra's inner south and Civic. The street network, building outlines and terrain come from the datasets below. All gameplay data and rendering dependencies are served locally.

Street network and OpenStreetMap outlines

© OpenStreetMap contributors. The local road and OSM outline database is available under the Open Database License 1.0. The original data was obtained through the official OpenStreetMap map API. The transformed snapshot is available as canberra.json, with source hashes and import details. The independent Civic/lake overlay adds the source road connection across both bridges, mapped Centre buildings and the lake shoreline with island holes. It also retains its pinned OSM API source hashes and ODbL terms. The additional road profile sidecar retains exact cached OSM service/access tags so driveways and parking aisles receive smaller presentation widths and are excluded from ambient public traffic.

Offline address search

© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The local address index contains 3,446 explicitly tagged records from the bounded OSM snapshot. Some points are approximate building centres. Missing house numbers are never invented or interpolated; coverage is incomplete. Search and play need no remote geocoder.

ACT building footprints

© Australian Capital Territory. ACTGOV_BUILDING_FOOTPRINTS, used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Footprints have been projected, simplified in presentation and supplemented with OSM outlines. The dataset is incomplete and does not establish current building appearance.

Additional building outlines

Microsoft GlobalML Building Footprints, used under the Community Data License Agreement Permissive 2.0. Machine-derived satellite outlines supplement ACT and OSM data after polygon-overlap checks. Heights remain estimates. The original inner-south snapshot has 12,572 source-derived outlines; the Civic overlay adds OSM outlines after duplicate checks.

Terrain

© Australian Capital Territory, CC BY 4.0. The current 513 × 513 terrain grid is interpolated from ACT's 2020 LiDAR-derived two-metre contours, with approximately 12-metre horizontal cells. It is not the native one-metre survey DEM. Elevations use AHD metres relative to a 550-metre datum. See data notices and numerical validation and limitations. The separate 259 × 157 northern grid uses the same ACT contour source at about 25-metre spacing, blended into the base. These are contour interpolations, not surveyed bridge decks. The lake uses the normal 555.93 m AHD design level; bridge deck profiles are original height estimates. The initial Mapzen terrain has been superseded; its historical attribution is retained.

Street references

The school, Manuka, Kingston and Red Hill streetscapes were compared with Street View samples. Landmark references include Parliament House (March 2015), Old Parliament House (March 2026), Manuka Oval (September 2020) and a partly obscured Kingston approach (March 2026), alongside official architectural facts. Original procedural models reflect observed architectural cues, paving, fencing and canopy forms. No Google imagery or textures are bundled. These remain approximate models, not photogrammetry.

Rendering, vehicles and fonts

Three.js r160, © the three.js authors, MIT licence. Original fictional procedural vehicles reuse this repository's car-arcade model factory. Atkinson Hyperlegible by the Braille Institute, SIL Open Font License. Interface, driving rules and streetscape details are original work for this project.

Landmark models

Original simplified models place Parliament House, Old Parliament House, Manuka Oval, Kingston Powerhouse, Manuka Pool and Canberra Centre at mapped coordinates. Formal forecourts and the oval field keep clear of procedural trees. Roofs, marble frontage, flag mast, stands, floodlights and industrial forms are interpretations of the references, with lightweight skyline models. Manuka Pool uses the exact cached OSM swimming-basin and site polygons, with an enclosed boundary, a passable gate and original heritage-style entrance detail. Wall materials, gate placement, paving and landscaping are approximate interpretations of public heritage and architectural references. No new pool Street View inspection is claimed. No Street View photos or textures are bundled.

Street activities and buses

Twenty Manuka shops and the Civic noodle restaurant use explicitly tagged OSM retail locations and matched source footprints, with original parody names such as Coals. Frontage positions are estimates derived from the footprints and adjacent public streets. Their fictional till robberies, food, prices and payouts do not describe the real businesses. The priority Lambzhou 1919 room uses original timber, seating and open-kitchen details informed by two official Canberra restaurant interior photographs. Its facade and floorplan are estimates, with no photo, logo or mural assets bundled. Gym, supplies and job markers remain fictional activities. Two original unbranded buses follow fictional ambient circuits on the real public-road graph; route numbers and timetables are not reproduced. Drivers, pedestrians and officers are fictional adults. The inherited car interiors use a left-side seat and wheel despite the left-hand road traffic.

Rooms and neighbourhoods

House rooms, shop interiors and the Centre concourse are original plausible layouts, generated only when entered. They are not surveyed private interiors or an exact mall floorplan. Gardens and backyards derive from building and road relationships, not cadastral boundaries. Telopea fencing follows the mapped school site with estimated openings at actual footpath crossings.

What is approximate

Building heights, roof forms, facades, vegetation placement, parked vehicles and road furniture are visual interpretations. Pedestrians are fictional adults with simplified walking and car avoidance; they do not represent real people. The school uses real building outlines with approximate architectural details informed by street references. Traffic uses simplified left-hand routing and spacing; decorative signals do not control traffic. This is not a Street View reconstruction, a current road-condition survey or a driving simulator.

Targets Intel N100 / 8 GB with low rendering settings. Actual target-device performance has not been measured. The playable area covers the inner south and a northern corridor to Civic. The complete lake polygon extends beyond the western drivable boundary. Other Canberra districts remain outside this preview.
