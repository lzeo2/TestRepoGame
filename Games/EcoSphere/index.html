EcoSphere

Sealed terrarium, your rules. Balance sunlight and water so plants and grazers thrive for 40 days. Sun too fierce burns the greens, constant rain drowns them, and grazers starve without them.

Controls
  • Sunlight: press S to cycle Off / Low / Mid / Full, or tap the Sun button.
  • Water: press W to cycle Off / Drip / Shower / Storm, or tap the Water button.
  • Pause: press P or the Pause button.
  • Restart: press N or use the New run button, any time.
  • Keyboard and touch both work. Plants are scored by size, grazers by count, day by day.
