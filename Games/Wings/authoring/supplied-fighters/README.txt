WINGS — BASE MODELS

30 unique original vehicles:20 aircraft shared by both modes +10vessels.

Load models.json, then models/<slug>.glb. Each self-contained base GLB includes its materials/textures. Optional rigs/<slug>.json lists rigid transform nodes and rest transforms. roster_lookup.json maps20standard and30Factions slots to the same30models.

Meters; right-handed +Y up,+Z forward,+X right. Origin: nominal aircraft body center or vessel waterline center. Preserve node hierarchy/rest transforms. No renderer-specific code, animation clips, physics, collision mesh, real weapon fit or game registration is included. Materials are standard glTF metallic-roughness.

This is original neutral-livery visual art, not an engineering reconstruction or claim of manufacturer endorsement/real operational capability. Public overall proportions are approximate where noted. Native Wings compatibility/performance and upstream game rights remain separate integration checks.

Editable Blender sources, alternate LODs and QA previews were retained outside this intentionally minimal package.
