Tanks and Soldiers

Capture Harbor, Relay and Depot. Hold posts to drain enemy tickets. You are one soldier, not a free camera.

Ready at base: tank northeast, jet west, ship at the pier east. Walk to a vehicle and press E. Friendly crews hold these vehicles for your first boarding.

Touch: hold/drag MOVE, drag LOOK with another finger; hold FIRE to shoot. BOOST sprints or boosts aircraft. BOARD enters/exits nearby vehicles; SQUAD orders infantry. Pause returns here. Movement pad drives vehicles; aircraft lift off with forward throttle, then release to coast and use LOOK for pitch. Keyboard also works; F fires. Touch does not need mouse capture.

Soldier: WASD, Shift, Space, R. Q orders up to three allied infantry to the nearest unheld post. Tank: WASD + mouse gunner.
Jet: W/S throttle, A/D turn, mouse or ↑/↓ pitch. Reach 42 m/s to lift off; slow below 65 m/s for a gentle landing.
Ship: WASD slow helm + mouse gun station. Stop at the dock to dismount. Stop tanks and landed jets before exiting.
Arrow keys also aim. Escape pauses; Resume briefly waits for safe mouse recapture, then needs a fresh click.

Soldier, tank, jet and boat models load locally with procedural fallbacks. Rendered appearance is not yet hardware-verified. 2.1 × 2.4 km coast; bounded active simulation. No multiplayer or backend.
