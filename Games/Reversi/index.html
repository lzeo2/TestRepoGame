Reversi

Trap the rival discs between two of yours to flip them. Highlighted squares show your legal moves; if neither side can move, the game ends and the biggest disc count wins. Tap a highlighted square to place a disc, or use the arrow keys to move the cursor and Enter to place one. You play black and move first.

Your discs: 2 Computer: 2
Rules and AI based on Reversi by Alexander Berson (MIT license).
